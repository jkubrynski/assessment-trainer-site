IFR GYM

Practise VOR holding patterns on an airliner-style navigation display, on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Fly to the fix, pick the right entry (Direct, Parallel or Teardrop) before you reach it, then fly the entry and one full hold using heading selections only. The debrief shows your track against the ideal pattern and scores the hold.

Support

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, its OS version and what you were doing when it happened.

Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.

Tips

  • Tap ? at any time to see the instructions again.
  • Your scores and settings are kept only on your device. Use RESET (tap twice) in the Score card to clear them.
  • The app pauses when you leave it and stays paused when you come back.

Privacy

IFR Gym collects no data. Read the privacy policy.

IFR Gym is a training and familiarisation tool, not for real-world navigation and not approved for flight training credit.

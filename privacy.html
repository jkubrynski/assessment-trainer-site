IFR GYM

Privacy policy for the IFR Gym app on iOS and Android.

In short: IFR Gym does not collect any data.

What the app collects

IFR Gym does not collect personal data, usage data, analytics, crash reports or identifiers of any kind. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores

The app saves your scores and your settings (holding fix type, wind and entry type) so they are still there next time. These stay on your device, in the app's own storage, and are not stored remotely: they are never sent to the developer or to any server.

Like other app data, they may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud or Google), which you control through your device settings.

Deleting the app removes this data from the device. The RESET button in the Score card clears your scores at any time.

Sharing

IFR Gym does not share any data with anyone, including third parties, because it has none to share.

Network

The app works fully offline. The only time it opens a web page is when you tap the privacy policy link, which opens this page in your browser.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
